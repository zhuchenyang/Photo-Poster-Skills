# Historical defaults and revisions

Maintenance reference only. Read when reviewing version history, not when generating a poster. These entries describe past decisions; the current SKILL.md and prompt-template.md govern execution. Historical scene-specific restrictions are not current requirements.

## Through v1.6 (archived verbatim)


Recovered from the referenced conversation: v1.1 material rules, priority order, 3:4 canvas, 50/50 regions, 30°–45° view, and 35%–45% lower-region negative space. The returned history did not contain the initial full skill. Photo-above/diorama-below and the interpretation of negative space as lower-region area are explicit implementation defaults, not verbatim recovered agreement. Follow a user correction to these defaults.

Version 1.2 incorporates the user's later requirement for professional placement and generous four-sided breathing room. It supersedes the fixed negative-space percentage when that percentage conflicts with a spacious composition; the margin and size ranges are practical starting guides, not claimed historical measurements.

Version 1.3 makes scene-specific English editorial copy and a left-text/right-model layout the default, with user overrides for text-free output or another language. Four-sided breathing room and material fidelity remain mandatory.

Version 1.4 originally required water-only volumes beneath water. The user's later correction supersedes that restriction: the base follows natural terrain continuity, with plausible soil, rock and underwater structure allowed, while rock edging around water remains prohibited.

Version 1.5 prioritizes first-pass model scale and a visibly thick cutaway base while preserving natural terrain and water continuity.

Version 1.6 preserves an established upper crop by extending missing image area when necessary to achieve the exact 50/50 split. Its shadow guidance uses natural contact shadow to express volume, weight and lifted layering.

## v1.7

Generalizes subject preservation, material fidelity, dimensional reconstruction, copy and review across photographic subjects using one observation-driven workflow. Replaces mandatory terrain cutaways with source-appropriate volume and structure. Consolidates water-specific rules into material and connection fidelity. Replaces the blanket stone-edging rejection with a prohibition on invented additions while preserving existing features. Aligns material guidance in the template and entrypoint, including genuinely uniform materials and colors. Removes fixed scene-to-mood associations. Retains poster format, layout, breathing room, camera style and natural shadows. Moves historical defaults out of runtime instructions.

## v1.8

Clarifies that foreground volumetric materials need readable depth in the base or exposed side section. At the user's request, foreground water must show a perceptible below-surface water column while preserving observed color, clarity and turbidity, continuous substrate and existing composition margins. Depth is artistically inferred, not measured; transparency, pure-water blocks and decorative rims are not required. Updates both generation guidance and review criteria.
